## Revisions made during the review

- The source's open ($12,000,000,000 after a skull) failed the brief's no-amounts rule and Unacceptable "Shocking or distasteful content". It was replaced by a typed line and a particle sphere.
- The source's "Introducing" title assembled from glitching pixels. A strobing version would fail Quality "aggressive flashing/effects". The film uses crisp type with a steady particle ring, and the only glitch is a 2-frame slice offset with no flash.
- Turning the particle figure into the ghost mark would have added an effect to the mark, which the press kit forbids. The real lockup file now travels and grows unaltered instead.
- An earlier cut held captions on screen for only 1–2 s (typed on). That was ambiguous under Quality "indecipherable", so captions now appear whole on their beat and hold to the next scene.

## Preconditions before upload (advertiser level; the creative cannot fix these)

Each one is a blocker until it is done. None is a reason to soften the creative. Nothing has been uploaded, posted or
scheduled.

1. **Landing page.**
   - As read on 2026-11-08, https://make.fun/:
     - opens a full-screen first-visit "Hey, we’re in beta!" sheet. Quality: "The landing page should not contain disruptive pop ups"
     - is titled "make.fun: AI agent bounties paid in USDC on Solana"
     - leads with "AI agents build software for cash rewards."
     - prints dollar amounts
     - lists Trading and Forecasting bounties. Quality: "The landing page must … be compliant with all ads policies"
   - **Fix:** suppress the beta sheet for ad traffic. Then either change the title and hero away from cash and USDC, or
     get X's written confirmation that the page is acceptable under the Financial services and Gambling policies.
   - Until one of those happens, the ad does not run to this URL.
2. **Financial services certification.**
   - make.fun pays bounties in USDC on Solana (its Terms: "Rewards are stated in US dollars and paid in USDC on Solana").
   - X restricts "cryptocurrency products and services" and requires "a prior authorization from X by getting certified".
     Its Blockchain Games rule covers "games involving NFT or cryptocurrency tokens where such can be won or earned".
   - **Action:** apply for X's certification, or get X's written classification that it does not apply, before launch.
   - Exclude Belgium, Greece, Qatar, Russia, Singapore, Slovenia and Ukraine, which X lists as "not available for
     advertising cryptocurrency & DeFi products and services".
3. **Gambling classification and country exclusions.**
   - make.fun is free and ranked by graded skill, but its board hosts forecasting and trading-simulation bounties.
   - X's Gambling policy covers "forecasts" and "Games or apps played for money".
   - **Exclude Canada** unless X pre-authorizes it ("Games are free to play but result in the payment of money or a prize
     of value." … "Prior authorization from X is required.").
   - **Exclude India** ("Ads promoting online money games are prohibited across India", skill games included).
   - Get X's classification before any other market where the gambling reading is in doubt.
4. **Age.** Target 18+ only. make.fun's Terms require "18 or older", and X prohibits marketing gambling products to
   minors.
5. **Contest law.** Get legal sign-off that the bounties are lawful skill contests in every targeted market. X:
   "comply with all applicable laws and regulations". make.fun: "Bounties are void where the law prohibits them."
6. **Account.** Run only from make.fun's own ads account (@makefunnyai), after X's ads-account approval. No creator or
   affiliate is paid to post it, which would bring in the Paid Partnerships policy and its own disclosure.
7. **Rights to made assets.** Confirm that the accounts used grant commercial use of their outputs:
   - ElevenLabs (ad 4's voice)
   - Google Lyria (ad 4's music)
8. **Destination.** The card or CTA URL is exactly https://make.fun/, with no redirect or tracking hop that changes the
   destination after review.
