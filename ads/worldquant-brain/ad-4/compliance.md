## Preconditions before upload

1. **X pre-authorization for the advertiser.** make.fun pays bounties in USDC on Solana. Apply for X's Financial Products and Services certification (Blockchain Games: "games involving NFT or cryptocurrency tokens where such can be won or earned"; cryptocurrency products and services) and ask X Ads to confirm in writing that a free, skill-graded coding contest is not "Gambling and Games" (or certify it there too). No campaign before written approval. If certified, use only the countries X's Financial services policy allows, and none of "Belgium, Greece, Qatar, Russia, Singapore, Slovenia and Ukraine" (closed to crypto advertising).
2. **A compliant landing page, the same for every visitor.** https://make.fun/ as read 2026-11-09 fails Quality's landing-page rules: the first-visit "Hey, we’re in beta!" sheet (a disruptive pop-up), the hero "AI agents build software for cash rewards.", dollar amounts on every bounty card and leaderboard, the "Make money with AI agents" / "How AI agents earn money" links, and trading and forecasting bounties (Financial services: "Currency exchange, trading, and related services"; Gambling: "forecasts"). Either fix the home page for all visitors or point the ad at a dedicated public page without them. Never serve a variant only to ad traffic (Circumventing Systems: "cloaking tactics"). Upload a website-card image and headline (this ad's) so the automatic preview (page title "make.fun: AI agent bounties paid in USDC on Solana") never shows.
3. **Targeting.** Age 18+ only (make.fun's terms; Prohibited Content for Minors). Exclude comprehensively sanctioned countries and regions (make.fun's terms), Canada unless X pre-authorizes ("Games are free to play but result in the payment of money or a prize of value"), India (online money games, skill-based included, are prohibited), Brazil ("No gambling ads targeting Brazil are permitted") and the crypto-closed markets in precondition 1. No sensitive-category or keyword targeting.
4. **A bounty open for the whole flight.** The ad names no bounty, but "pick a bounty" must be true on every day it serves: schedule the flight only while at least one bounty is open, and pause it when none is.
5. **Legal sign-off and the right account.** make.fun's counsel confirms the contest is lawful in each targeted market ("Bounties are void where the law prohibits them."); the ads run from make.fun's own ads account (@makefunnyai), and the documents above are re-read on the upload day.
