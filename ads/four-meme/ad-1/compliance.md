## Revisions made in review

- The plan's payoff had the ghost holding up the magenta star disc from the mark. make.fun's own logo code names that
  part “coin”, and a mascot holding up a coin as the payoff can read as “win a token” (Financial services; the
  brief's no-crypto rule). The payoff became the ghost looking back at the three lit worlds and walking on; the disc
  appears only inside the untouched lockup.
- The source's green “turns green” light (a crypto price cue) became make.fun magenta, drawn only as light along
  each world's seams.
- The vault door stays shut, so nothing implies a secret was given up or a bounty won.
