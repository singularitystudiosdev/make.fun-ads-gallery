## Revisions made in review

- The brief's draft hook "Every red check gives way to green" was changed before production to "Every red check comes
  with its fix": "every … gives way to green" reads as a promise that every failed entry ends up passing (Deceptive ›
  Impractical Outcomes), and the real UI marks a passed stage with a black tick, not green. The new line restates
  make.fun's own "The status card lists each failed check and its fix."
- The source's phone showed a third party's stock-exchange listing (Financial; Unauthorized References; a real event).
  The phone shows make.fun's own Rejected card, staged on the real frontend and labelled "Example entry".
- The card's last two stage rows (Challenge, Paid) are removed, so it shows no payout.
- A hard cut from the bright card insert into the dark slump measured a large luma jump; it was given a 9-frame dip
  (Quality › Media: "aggressive flashing/effects").
- The ghost never holds the star disc from the mark (make.fun's logo code names it "coin").
