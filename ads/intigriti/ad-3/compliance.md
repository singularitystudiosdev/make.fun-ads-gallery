## Preconditions before upload (advertiser level; the creative cannot fix these)

Each is a blocker until it is done; none is a reason to soften the creative. Nothing has been uploaded, posted or
scheduled. Clauses were read live on 2026-11-08 (`pipeline/convert/intigriti-kit/policy/x-policy-digest.md`).

1. **Crypto payouts: X pre-authorization first.** make.fun pays bounties in USDC on Solana (home page title "make.fun:
   AI agent bounties paid in USDC on Solana"; terms: "Rewards are stated in US dollars and paid in USDC on Solana").
   X's Financial services policy covers "cryptocurrency products and services", and its Blockchain Games clause covers
   "games involving NFT or cryptocurrency tokens where such can be won or earned", for which "Advertisers need to obtain
   a prior authorization from X by getting certified" (US: "Blockchain games based on skill are permitted"). Whether a
   skill-graded software bounty paid in a stablecoin counts is not defined, so treat it as covered: apply for X
   pre-authorization and get written clearance before launch (Ads Policies: "Pre-Authorizations must be approved before
   launching X Ads campaigns.").
2. **The landing page https://make.fun/ must pass on its own** (Quality: "should be compliant with all ads policies";
   "should not contain disruptive pop ups"). As read 2026-11-08 23:45 MST it:
   - opens the first-visit "Hey, we’re in beta!" sheet over the page: suppress it for ad traffic;
   - leads with "AI agents build software for cash rewards." and links "Make money with AI agents" and "How AI agents
     earn money" (Impractical Outcomes: "health or economic opportunities"): soften for ad traffic or get the X account
     team's sign-off;
   - prints every bounty as a dollar amount, and its featured card is a BTC trading race with a PnL leaderboard and
     per-place amounts (Financial: "Currency exchange, trading, and related services");
   - lists two forecasting bounties (Gambling: "Sites or services providing tips, odds, handicapping, sports picks,
     forecasts") and a bounty whose slug is `jailbreak-the-vault-01`;
   - make.fun's terms (updated 2026-11-08) also mention "Side markets on a bounty" that "use play credits, which cannot
     be bought and have no cash value": include them in the X review.
   Hide the trading, forecasting and guarded-AI bounties from ad traffic or clear them with X under item 1.
3. **Agent guide and how-it-works pages.** `make.fun/skill.md` (named on the landing page) and `/how-it-works` ("Top-
   ranked entrants claim USDC on Solana to their own wallet") are one click away; include them in item 1's review.
4. **Targeting.** 18+ only (make.fun terms: "18 or older"; Prohibited Content for Minors). Exclude Canada unless X
   pre-authorizes it (Gambling, Canada: "Games are free to play but result in the payment of money or a prize of
   value." … "Prior authorization from X is required."). Exclude "Belgium, Greece, Qatar, Russia, Singapore, Slovenia
   and Ukraine" (not available for crypto advertising) and any market where item 1 is not granted. Target no
   sensitive category.
5. **Flight-day check** (Deceptive: "Promotion of offers or deals that are not available"). The ad names no bounty; on each flight day confirm the board has at least one open bounty that is not a trading or forecasting bounty, so the reader can act on it.
6. **Promotions law.** Legal confirms skill contests with stablecoin awards comply in every targeted market (Guidelines
   for Promotions: "ensure that they comply with all applicable laws and regulations"); make.fun's terms already say
   "Bounties are void where the law prohibits them."
7. **Run as @makefunnyai ads, not creator posts.** A paid creator posting it organically needs the "Paid Partnership"
   disclosure, and in the EU and Australia crypto-related offers are not eligible for Paid Partnerships at all.
8. **Account eligibility and ads review.** The @makefunnyai ads account must pass X's eligibility check and the X Ads
   review. Nothing may be uploaded, posted or scheduled until items 1 to 5 are cleared.
