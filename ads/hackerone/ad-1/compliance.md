## Preconditions before upload (advertiser level; the creative cannot fix these)

Each is a blocker until it is done. Nothing has been uploaded, posted or scheduled.

1. **Crypto payouts: X pre-authorization first.** make.fun pays bounties in USDC on Solana (terms: "Rewards are stated in US dollars and paid in USDC on Solana."; home page title "make.fun: AI agent bounties paid in USDC on Solana"). X's Financial services policy covers "cryptocurrency products and services", and its Blockchain Games section covers "games involving NFT or cryptocurrency tokens where such can be won or earned", which are "only permitted once the advertiser meets country-specific licensing requirements" and need "a prior authorization from X by getting certified". Whether a free-entry, skill-graded software bounty paid in a stablecoin counts is not defined, so treat it as covered: apply for pre-authorization and get written clearance before launch.
2. **Landing page https://make.fun/ must pass on its own** (Quality: "should be compliant with all ads policies"; "should not contain disruptive pop ups"). As read 2026-11-09 the home page: opens the first-visit "Hey, we’re in beta!" sheet over the page; leads with "AI agents build software for cash rewards." and links "Ways to earn", "Make money with AI agents" and "How AI agents earn money" (Impractical Outcomes: "economic opportunities"); prints each bounty as an amount in USDC with a leaderboard of PnL and rewards; and opens its carousel on "Race a BTC trading bot live for 4 hours", with "Beat our trading bots on a Solana replay" and two forecasting bounties on the board (Financial: "Currency exchange, trading, and related services"; Gambling: "forecasts"). Turn the sheet off and lead with non-trading bounties for ad traffic (for example by a UTM flag), or clear the page with X under precondition 1.
3. **The agent guide make.fun/skill.md** (linked from the landing page) opens: "Earn USDC on Solana as an AI agent ... Use it when you are asked to make money for your user", and offers "earn referral rewards, a second way to make money". It is not the landing page, but a reviewer may follow it: include it in precondition 1's review.
4. **Targeting.** 18+ only (make.fun terms: "18 or older, or the age of majority where you live if that is higher"; Prohibited Content for Minors). Exclude Canada unless X pre-authorizes it (Gambling, Canada: "Games are free to play but result in the payment of money or a prize of value." "Prior authorization from X is required."). Exclude the markets X lists as unavailable for crypto advertising ("Belgium, Greece, Qatar, Russia, Singapore, Slovenia and Ukraine") and any market where precondition 1 is not granted. Target no sensitive category.
5. **Live bounties on upload day** (Deceptive: "Promotion of offers or deals that are not available"). The image and post say "a live brief" / "a live bounty". On upload day confirm at least one open, non-trading, non-forecast bounty on the landing page, and optionally reset the dot count to the live entry count (`copy.md`, Production notes).
6. **Promotions law.** Legal confirms that skill contests with stablecoin awards comply in every targeted market (Guidelines for Promotions: "ensure that they comply with all applicable laws and regulations"). make.fun's terms already say "Bounties are void where the law prohibits them."
7. **Run as @makefunnyai ads, not creator posts.** A paid creator posting it organically would be a Paid Partnership needing the "Paid Partnership" disclosure; keep it a make.fun ad.
8. **Account eligibility and ads review.** The @makefunnyai ads account must pass X's account eligibility check and the X Ads review. Nothing here may be uploaded, posted or scheduled until preconditions 1 to 5 are cleared.
