## Preconditions before upload (advertiser level; the creative cannot fix these)

Each is a blocker until it is done; none is a reason to soften the creative. Nothing has been uploaded, posted or
scheduled. Clauses were read live on 2026-11-08 (see `pipeline/convert/bugcrowd-kit/policy/x-policy-digest.md`).

1. **Crypto payouts: X pre-authorization first.** make.fun pays bounties in USDC on Solana (home page title "make.fun:
   AI agent bounties paid in USDC on Solana"; terms: "Rewards are stated in US dollars and paid in USDC on Solana").
   X's Financial services policy covers "cryptocurrency products and services", and its Blockchain Games clause covers
   "games involving NFT or cryptocurrency tokens where such can be won or earned", which are "only permitted once the
   advertiser meets country-specific licensing requirements" and need "a prior authorization from X by getting
   certified" (US: "Blockchain games based on skill are permitted"). Whether a free-entry, skill-graded software bounty
   paid in a stablecoin is a "blockchain game" is not defined, so treat it as covered: apply at
   https://ads.x.com/en/help/ads-pre-authorization and get written clearance before launch.
2. **Landing page https://make.fun/ must pass on its own** (Quality: "should be compliant with all ads policies";
   "should not contain disruptive pop ups"). As read 2026-11-08 the home page:
   - opens the first-visit "Hey, we’re in beta!" sheet over the page: turn it off for ad traffic (for example by a UTM
     flag) or land on a URL without it;
   - leads with "AI agents build software for cash rewards." and links "Make money with AI agents" and "How AI agents
     earn money" guides, an economic-opportunity pitch (Impractical Outcomes: "health or economic opportunities"):
     soften for ad traffic or get the X account team's sign-off;
   - prints every bounty as an amount "in USDC", covered by item 1's authorization;
   - lists "Beat our trading bots on a Solana replay" and "Race a BTC trading bot live for 4 hours" (Financial:
     "Currency exchange, trading, and related services") and two forecasting bounties (Gambling: "Sites or services
     providing tips, odds, handicapping, sports picks, forecasts"). Hide them from ad traffic, or clear them with X as
     part of item 1.
3. **The agent guide make.fun/skill.md** (one click from the landing page, and named in the creatives' prompt) opens
   with "Earn USDC on Solana as an AI agent" and "Use it when you are asked to make money for your user". It is not
   the landing page, but reviewers may follow it: include it in item 1's review.
4. **Targeting.** 18+ only (make.fun's terms: "18 or older"; Prohibited Content for Minors). Exclude Canada unless X
   pre-authorizes it (Gambling, Canada: "Games are free to play but result in the payment of money or a prize of
   value ... Prior authorization from X is required."). Exclude the markets X lists as unavailable for crypto
   advertising ("Belgium, Greece, Qatar, Russia, Singapore, Slovenia and Ukraine") and any market where item 1 is not
   granted. Target no sensitive category.
5. **Live-bounty check on upload day** (Deceptive: "Promotion of offers or deals that are not available"). The laptop screen shows "Write make.fun's tagline" (closes 2026-11-11 01:00 UTC) and the header count "Search 9 live bounties, 167 entries"; the post text says peers' agents are "already on the leaderboard". On upload day re-capture the screen on a bounty that is open, non-trading and non-forecast, re-read the counts, and re-render (`copy.md`, "Swapping the bounty").
6. **Promotions law.** Legal confirms skill contests with stablecoin awards comply in every targeted market (Guidelines
   for Promotions: "ensure that they comply with all applicable laws and regulations"). make.fun's terms already say
   "Bounties are void where the law prohibits them."
7. **Run as @makefunnyai ads, not creator posts.** A paid creator posting it organically needs the "Paid Partnership"
   disclosure, and in the EU, UK and Australia crypto-related offers are not eligible for Paid Partnerships at all.
8. **Account eligibility and ads review.** The @makefunnyai ads account must pass X's account eligibility check and the
   X Ads review. Nothing here may be uploaded, posted or scheduled until items 1 to 5 are cleared.
