## Preconditions before upload

These are advertiser-level conditions that no change to the creative can fix. Clear all of them before any campaign goes live. Clauses were read live on 2026-11-08; the policy texts are saved in `pipeline/convert/shipd-kit/policy/`.

1. **Crypto payouts need X pre-authorization.** make.fun pays bounties in USDC on Solana. Its home page title reads “AI agent bounties paid in USDC on Solana”, and how-it-works says “Top-ranked entrants claim USDC on Solana to their own wallet.” X's Financial Products and Services policy covers “cryptocurrency products and services”. Its Blockchain Games clause covers “games involving NFT or cryptocurrency tokens where such can be won or earned” and says “Advertisers need to obtain a prior authorization from X by getting certified.” The policy does not settle whether a free, skill-graded coding competition paid in a stablecoin is a blockchain game, so treat it as one. Get certified, and get written clearance from the X account team, before launch.
2. **The landing page `https://make.fun/` must pass on its own.** Quality says it “should be compliant with all ads policies” and “should not contain disruptive pop ups”. As read on 2026-11-08 (about 00:00 UTC on 11-09), the home page has these problems:
   - **Reward and income wording.** The headline is “AI agents build software for cash rewards.” Every bounty card lists a dollar reward “in USDC”, and links include “Ways to earn”, “Make money with AI agents” and “How AI agents earn money” (Deceptive: “economic opportunities”).
   - **Trading bounties** (Financial: “Currency exchange, trading”): “Race a BTC trading bot live for 4 hours” and “Beat our trading bots on a Solana replay”.
   - **Forecasting bounties** (Gambling: “forecasts”): “Predict tomorrow's top 10 trending Roblox games” and “Predict the next 5 trading days' moves of 9 Nasdaq stocks”.
   - **A first-visit pop-up**: the “Hey, we're in beta!” sheet.

   Fix these on the page the ad sends people to, or send ad traffic to a dedicated page that has no amounts, crypto, trading or forecasting bounties and no pop-up. Either change must be live before review and must stay live, which also satisfies Circumventing Systems. The alternative is to get the current page approved as part of item 1.
3. **Open bounties on upload day.** These ads name no bounty, but the post text says “Point your agent at a real bounty” and “Free to enter”, so a non-financial, non-forecast bounty must be open whenever the ads run. As read on 2026-11-08, the open non-financial bounties were:
   - “Find the shortest delivery route”, ending 2026-11-09 03:00 UTC.
   - “Make a guarded AI reveal its secret”, ending 2026-11-09 20:00 UTC.
   - “Design make.fun's sticker” and “Write make.fun's tagline”, both ending 2026-11-10 20:00 UTC.

   Pause the campaign whenever none is open.
4. **Targeting.**
   - Set an 18+ age gate (Prohibited Content for Minors).
   - Exclude Canada unless X pre-authorizes it. The Gambling policy lists “Games are free to play but result in the payment of money or a prize of value” there, with “Prior authorization from X is required”.
   - Exclude India, where “Online money games can be both skill based games … or chance based games” and are prohibited.
   - Exclude the markets closed to crypto advertising: Belgium, Greece, Qatar, Russia, Singapore, Slovenia and Ukraine.
   - Exclude any market where item 1's certification is not granted.
5. **Promotions law.** Each bounty page carries its full rules, and entering is free. Legal must confirm that skill contests paying crypto prizes comply in every targeted market (Guidelines for Promotions: “ensure that they comply with all applicable laws and regulations”).
6. **Run these as make.fun ads, not creator posts.** Post them as Promoted posts from make.fun's own X account. The site's social link is `https://x.com/makefunai`; one page render showed a different handle in the footer, so confirm the account before setup. If a creator ever posts one organically for pay:
   - The “Paid Partnership” disclosure and the FTC Endorsement Guides apply.
   - In the EU, the UK and Australia, crypto-related offers are not eligible for Paid Partnerships at all.
   - Never create a stand-in “CS student” account (Authenticity: “manufactured identities”).
7. **Account eligibility and ads review.** The make.fun ads account must pass X's account-eligibility check and the X Ads review. Nothing here has been uploaded, posted or scheduled, and nothing may be until items 1–4 are cleared.
8. **Music licence** (ads 1 and 2). The music was generated for these ads with Google Lyria and ElevenLabs Music. Before a paid run, confirm that the plans on the accounts used grant commercial-use rights for generated music in paid advertising. This makes the ToS rights warranty in rows 1–2 airtight.
9. **Generated-media disclosure laws outside X.** X's policies read on 2026-11-08 ask for no disclosure of generated media in a non-deceptive ad, and by the brief's rule the creative carries no such label. Some jurisdictions have their own transparency rules for generated video; the EU AI Act's transparency obligations are an example. Have legal confirm those rules before targeting such markets, and exclude a market where a label would be required.

Specific to this ad:

- The ad names no single bounty, so it does not expire with one. It still needs at least one open, non-financial, non-forecast bounty on upload day (Precondition 3).
- The ad is silent on purpose, as its source was, and the burned-in text carries it. If X's review flags the silent track, add a soft room tone. Do not add a voiceover.
