pre-authorization is a fail. Creative, landing page and advertiser eligibility are judged separately.

(`out/r1/contact.png`) and the round-1 animatic (15.5 s, 30 fps, 540 x 676), measured below. The verdict tables are

what changed, which rows that clears, which stay open, and the re-checks on the final render.

## Fixes for every fail (priority order)

1. **Landing route (clears the pop-up, relevance, consistency, forecast/trading, wealth-claim rows; does not clear the category).** Serve ad traffic to a campaign route for `https://dev.make.fun/bounty/close-the-books-01` that (a) does not mount `beta-welcome` (for example set `localStorage["makefun:beta-welcome:v1"]` before mount, or a build with the dialog off); test on a fresh profile; (b) carries a title/H1/lead with no "cash rewards", "Make money", "earn USDC", "AI agent bounties paid in USDC on Solana"; (c) lists no Trading, Forecasting, memecoin, tokenized-stock or dropshipping bounty; (d) says in the page what the contest is: a free, skill-scored coding competition where entries are ranked and not every entry places. Serve the same route to X's reviewer and to live traffic. The bounty page itself still shows "$1,325 · 10 places paid"; that is why Fix 7 is the durable fix.
2. **Film: staged win and outcome claims (Deceptive, Impractical Outcomes).** Do not open on the win (frame 0 is "You won 1st place"): open on the board and put the win last. Add a persistent small legend from frame 0 to the end, e.g. "Demo. Results are staged." If the advertiser keeps no on-screen label, these two rows stay failed and the response to X must say so. Reword: "BOUNTIES YOU CAN WIN" to "Bounties to enter"; "WIN THE BOUNTY" to "See where you place" (or "Finish first"); "AIM FOR FIRST" to "Aim high"; end line "WIN BOUNTIES." to "Enter bounties." (the advertiser's chosen line "win bounties" is the one most exposed to the Impractical Outcomes clause). Show the three rival rows with the win so it reads as one outcome among several. Put "ranked by score; not every entry places" in the post copy.
3. **Film: indecipherable and cropped frames (Quality, Media).** Cap the pre-letter scramble on each caption tile to its last 0.15-0.2 s and show only blank tiles or the final letter in between, never random glyphs on screen for whole frames; the same for the wordmark (no "2a" frame) and for the link and sha tiles (show characters landing in order, not a block of unrelated characters); keep both zoomed fields (1.28x) fully inside the frame so "https://github.com/you/month-close" and the 40-char sha are not clipped at the right edge. Cut the number of whole-screen turns where a turn is not carrying a step.
4. **Film: capitals and URLs (Editorial).** Set the caption tiles in sentence case ("Bounties to enter.") instead of all caps; the post copy must have no hashtag, no URL, at most one emoji, correct grammar. To remove the URL ambiguity, either accept it as UI and answer X with that, or crop the prompt box to "Read the skill file and enter the bounty..." and drop the zoom on the repo field.
5. **Film: stale board (MSA s.4, Deceptive).** Re-capture on launch day, or keep only bounties that outlive the flight, and use the "Close a month of books" bounty only; drop the AlgoTune, Markdown and date-parsing cards or relabel the board so it does not say "Live bounties N" with ended items. Put no count or countdown in the post copy.
6. **Post copy.** Say what the product is and what it pays: a free-to-enter skill competition on make.fun where rewards are paid in USDC to the places that beat the house entries. This restores creative/destination consistency without a money figure on screen.
7. **Advertiser and category (the blocker).** Submit X's financial pre-authorization for **cryptocurrency products and services** and for **Blockchain Games** (separate requests; one does not extend to the other) naming make.fun's skill-graded USDC bounties, with written confirmation of how X classes them; target the United States only unless the certification covers other countries, and exclude Belgium, Greece, Qatar, Russia, Singapore, Slovenia and Ukraine.
