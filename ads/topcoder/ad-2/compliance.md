## Preconditions before upload (advertiser level; the creative cannot fix these)

Each one blocks upload until it is done. Nothing has been uploaded, posted or scheduled.

X does not say whether a free-entry, skill-graded software bounty paid in a stablecoin counts. Ambiguous counts as a fail, so treat it as covered: apply for certification and get written clearance before launch.
2. **The landing page https://make.fun/ must pass on its own** (Quality: "should be compliant with all ads policies"; "should not contain disruptive pop ups"). As read at 2026-11-09 08:41 UTC, the home page:
