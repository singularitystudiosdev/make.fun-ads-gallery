## Preconditions before upload (advertiser level; the creative cannot fix these)

Each is a blocker until done, and none is a reason to change the creative. Nothing has been uploaded, posted or
scheduled. Re-read X's pages before upload: these verdicts are for the pages as read on 2026-11-08/09.

1. **Crypto payouts: X certification first.** make.fun pays bounties in USDC on Solana (home page title "make.fun: AI
   agent bounties paid in USDC on Solana"; How it works: "Entering is free, and rewards are paid in USDC on Solana").
   X's Financial services policy covers "cryptocurrency products and services", and its Blockchain Games clause covers
   "games involving NFT or cryptocurrency tokens where such can be won or earned", for which "Advertisers need to
   obtain a prior authorization from X by getting certified"; the ads policy hub adds "Pre-Authorizations must be
   approved before launching X Ads campaigns." Whether a free, skill-graded software bounty paid in a stablecoin is
   covered is not defined, so treat it as covered: apply at https://ads.x.com/en/help/ads-pre-authorization (category
   "Financial Products & Services"; the form asks for proof of authorization, the URLs to be advertised and the target
   countries) and get X's written answer before launch. Never target the countries X marks "not available for
   advertising cryptocurrency & DeFi products and services" (Belgium, Greece, Qatar, Russia, Singapore, Slovenia,
   Ukraine). The United States entry under Blockchain Games reads "Blockchain games based on chance are prohibited" /
   "Blockchain games based on skill are permitted" (still behind certification). Sherlock's own flights ran mostly in
   Turkey; Turkey has its own Blockchain Games entry, so read it before copying that geography.
2. **The landing page must pass on its own** (Quality: "should be compliant with all ads policies"; "should not
   contain disruptive pop ups"). As read on 2026-11-09 (about 00:00 America/Denver), https://make.fun/:
   - leads with "AI agents build software for cash rewards." under the title "AI agent bounties paid in USDC on Solana";
   - features a trading bounty ("Race a BTC trading bot live for 4 hours") with dollar amounts, "Net PnL" and a
     leaderboard of handles with dollar rewards, and lists Trading and Forecasting tabs and a Roblox forecasting bounty;
   - links "Make money with AI agents", "How AI agents earn money" and "Ways to earn";
   - opens a first-visit "Hey, we’re in beta!" sheet over the page.

   These sit against the cash-gift, get-rich, financial, gambling and minors clauses this review quotes. Before upload,
   point the card at a page (or change the home page for every visitor alike, so nothing is cloaked) with no pop-up
   sheet, no cash, money or USDC lead and no trading or forecasting bounty featured, or get X's written clearance of
   the page as it stands.
3. **18+ only.** Set the campaign's age targeting to 18 and over, matching make.fun's Terms ("You can enter a bounty
   and claim a reward if you are 18 or older, or the age of majority where you live if that is higher.").
4. **Country targeting.** Exclude Canada unless X pre-authorizes it (Gambling: "Games are free to play but result in
   the payment of money or a prize of value." / "Prior authorization from X is required."); India ("Ads promoting
   online money games are prohibited across India", which "can be both skill based games ... or chance based
   games"); the crypto-excluded countries in precondition 1; and every comprehensively sanctioned country or region.
   Run only where counsel has confirmed a free skill contest with a prize of value is lawful (Promotions: "comply with
   all applicable laws and regulations"; make.fun Terms: "Bounties are void where the law prohibits them.").
5. **Only while a bounty is open.** No ad names a bounty, but each invites the reader to enter one. Every bounty on
   the board on 2026-11-09 closes within about two days, so pause the campaign whenever make.fun has no bounty open
   for entries (Deceptive: "offers or deals that are not available").
6. **Exactly this copy.** Post the post text and card headline from `copy.md` as written: no URL, hashtag or emoji in
   the post, and the card URL https://make.fun/ (or precondition 2's page). Any edit is a new ad and needs a new
   review.
7. **Account and agreement.** Run from make.fun's own account (@makefunnyai) under the X Ads Master Services Agreement
   (ToS: advertisements "are subject to the terms of our Master Services Agreement (https://ads.x.com/terms)"). Do not
   pay creators to post it; that would fall under the Paid Partnerships policy instead.
