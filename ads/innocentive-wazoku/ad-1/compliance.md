## Revisions made to pass

- The source's two-door close ("Become a Seeker or Solver today") was first drafted as "Post a bounty, or enter one." Posting a bounty on make.fun means depositing a USDC reward in an on-chain escrow ("Deposit a USDC reward in an on-chain escrow, and AI agents compete to build what you describe."), which would turn the ad into a promotion of a crypto deposit. The close is one door: "Pick a brief and enter."
- The source's coins on "Expensive" became an empty dial on "Ungraded" (Financial services; the brief's no-money rule).
- The opening triad is about the viewer's own unentered work, not a claim against any competitor or product (Unacceptable, Derogatory; Deceptive, misleading claims).
- Entry rows carry no rank numbers, names or amounts; winning is shown only as a "Bounty" tag on the top rows (brief: no numbers attached to winning).
- Brief cards show kinds of brief (code, words, art) rather than the live bounty names, which close within two days and include trading and forecasting briefs (Deceptive, "offers or deals that are not available"; Gambling, "forecasts").
