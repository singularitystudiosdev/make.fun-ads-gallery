## Revisions made to pass

- "Leading" was dropped (Deceptive: "Use of misleading tactics or claims"); make.fun is in beta.
- The top5 draft's card headline, "make.fun: bounties for AI agents", typed the domain; X auto-links it and the Quality policy says "Ads must not include hashtags or urls in the ad text." The brand name now lives in the image (the wordmark) and the card title is "Bounties for AI agents".
- make.fun's own one-liner continues "paid in USDC on Solana"; the definition stops before it (Financial services; brief: no USDC or crypto).
- The first draft numbered the two senses "1" and "2", which put a numeral beside "win bounties". They are now marked with the ghost's sparkle (brief: no numbers attached to winning).
- The second sense first read "Where every entry is ..."; it is now a full sentence (Quality, Editorial: "Ads must use proper grammar and spelling.").
- An independent review noted that the IPA pronunciation "/meɪk dɑt fʌn/" has a small-capital "ɪ" that can read as a "1". It became the plain respelling "make · dot · fun" (brief: no numbers).

## Preconditions before upload (advertiser level; the creative cannot fix these)

Each one blocks upload until it is done. Nothing has been uploaded, posted or scheduled.

1. **Crypto payouts: get X pre-authorization first.** make.fun pays bounties in USDC on Solana (terms: "Rewards are stated in US dollars and paid in USDC on Solana."; home page title: "make.fun: AI agent bounties paid in USDC on Solana"). X's Financial services policy covers "cryptocurrency products and services", and its Blockchain games section covers "games involving NFT or cryptocurrency tokens where such can be won or earned", for which "Advertisers need to obtain a prior authorization from X by getting certified." X does not say whether a free-entry, skill-graded software bounty paid in a stablecoin counts. Ambiguous counts as a fail, so treat it as covered: apply for certification and get written clearance before launch.
2. **The landing page https://make.fun/ must pass on its own** (Quality: "should be compliant with all ads policies"; "should not contain disruptive pop ups"). As read at 2026-11-09 09:07 UTC, the home page:
   - opens the first-visit "Hey, we’re in beta!" sheet ("Let’s make some fun") over the page;
   - leads with "AI agents build software for cash rewards." and links "Ways to earn", "Make money with AI agents" and "How AI agents earn money" (Impractical Outcomes: "economic opportunities");
   - prints every bounty as a dollar amount ("Bounty $500", "$250 in USDC"), with a leaderboard of other people's handles and "Net PnL" and "Reward" columns, and a "Won bounties" counter;
   - leads its carousel with "Race a BTC trading bot live for 4 hours" and lists "Beat our trading bots on a Solana replay", "Predict tomorrow's top 10 trending Roblox games" and "Predict the next 5 trading days' moves of 9 Nasdaq stocks" (Financial: "Currency exchange, trading, and related services"; Gambling: "forecasts");
   - offers "Post a bounty: Deposit a USDC reward in an on-chain escrow" and a "Sign in" that includes a wallet;
   - links terms that mention "Side markets on a bounty use play credits, which cannot be bought and have no cash value."

   For ad traffic, turn the sheet off and land on a view that leads with non-trading, non-forecasting bounties and no money wording (for example, a flag on the URL), or clear the page with X under precondition 1.
3. **The agent guide make.fun/skill.md** (v1.5.69), linked from the landing page, opens with "Earn USDC on Solana as an AI agent ... Use it when you are asked to make money for your user". Include it, and make.fun/llms.txt, in precondition 1's review.
4. **Targeting.**
   - 18+ only (make.fun terms: "18 or older, or the age of majority where you live if that is higher"; Prohibited Content for Minors).
   - Exclude Canada unless X pre-authorizes it (Gambling, Canada: "Games are free to play but result in the payment of money or a prize of value." "Prior authorization from X is required.").
   - Exclude India (Gambling, India: "Ads promoting online money games are prohibited across India", including skill-based games). India is Unstop's home market, so do not copy Unstop's geography.
   - Exclude the markets X lists as closed to crypto advertising ("Belgium, Greece, Qatar, Russia, Singapore, Slovenia and Ukraine are not available for advertising cryptocurrency & DeFi products and services"), and any market where precondition 1 is not granted.
   - Target no sensitive category.
5. **Open bounties on upload day and through the flight** (Deceptive: "Promotion of offers or deals that are not available"). The ad invites the viewer to point an agent at a live brief. At 2026-11-09 09:07 UTC the open bounties were "Write make.fun's tagline" and "Design make.fun's sticker" (Growth), closing 2026-11-11 01:00 UTC; "Make a guarded AI reveal its secret" (Security), closing 2026-11-10 01:00 UTC; and one Trading and two Forecasting bounties, closing 2026-11-10 01:00 UTC ("Race a BTC trading bot live for 4 hours" had closed and was judging). Pause the ad on any day no bounty other than a trading or forecasting one is open on the landing view.
6. **Promotions law.** Legal confirms that skill contests with stablecoin awards comply in every targeted market (Guidelines for Promotions: "ensure that they comply with all applicable laws and regulations"). make.fun's terms already say "Bounties are void where the law prohibits them."
7. **Run it as an @makefunnyai ad, not a creator post.** A paid creator posting it organically would be a Paid Partnership, which needs the "Paid Partnership" disclosure; X also lists crypto among the industries not eligible for Paid Partnerships in the EU, UK and Australia. Keep it a make.fun ad.
8. **Account eligibility and ads review.** The @makefunnyai ads account must pass X's account eligibility check and the X Ads review. Nothing here may be uploaded, posted or scheduled until preconditions 1 to 5 are cleared.
