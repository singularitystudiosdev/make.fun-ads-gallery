## Revisions made to reach a pass

- "The Gray Swan Arena Launches TODAY @ 1 PM EST!" was dropped (Quality: "excessive use of capitalization"; make.fun is not launching) and became "Bounties are live."
- "$40K in prizes", "No coding required" and "your best multi-turn exploits" (the brief; Deceptive; ToS §4 (iv)) were dropped.
- "push AI models to their limits" (someone else's model) became "push your AI agent to its limits"; "a shot at" (chance wording) became "compete for".
- The ember-red eyes (Unacceptable Content: "intimidating") became friendly magenta star eyes.
- "make.fun bounties" became "bounties" in the post text because X can auto-link "make.fun"; the brand stays in the image and the card's domain line.
- The source's 16:10 became 16:9, a ratio X lists. Image take 2 was rejected because its neon letters misspelled the brand.

## Preconditions before upload (advertiser level; the creative cannot fix these)

Each is a blocker until it is done; none is a reason to soften the creative. Nothing has been uploaded, posted or
scheduled. X's clauses were read live on 2026-11-08 (captures and digest: `pipeline/convert/gray-swan-arena-kit/policy/`);
make.fun's pages were read the same evening.

1. **Crypto payouts: X pre-authorization first.** make.fun pays bounties in USDC on Solana (page title "make.fun: AI
   agent bounties paid in USDC on Solana"; terms: "Rewards are stated in US dollars and paid in USDC on Solana."). X's
   Financial services policy restricts "cryptocurrency products and services" and defines "Blockchain games" as
   "games involving NFT or cryptocurrency tokens where such can be won or earned", which need "a prior authorization
   from >x< by getting certified" (US tab: "Blockchain games based on skill are permitted"). make.fun's terms say it is
   free to enter, decided by skill, and that it "has not issued and does not sell any coin or token", but whether X
   treats a skill bounty paid in a stablecoin as a blockchain game is undefined, so treat it as covered: get X's written
   clearance before launch.
2. **Landing page https://make.fun/ must pass on its own** (Quality: "should be compliant with all ads policies";
   "should not contain disruptive pop ups"). As read 2026-11-08 between 23:20 and 23:45 America/Denver the home page:
   - opens the first-visit "Hey, we’re in beta!" sheet: turn it off for ad traffic or land on a URL without it;
   - leads with "AI agents build software for cash rewards." and links "Make money with AI agents" and "How AI agents
     earn money" (Impractical Outcomes: "health or economic opportunities"): soften for ad traffic or get X's sign-off;
   - features in its hero "Race a BTC trading bot live for 4 hours" (cbBTC against USDC, "$200 in USDC", a net-PnL
     leaderboard with payout figures), lists "Beat our trading bots on a Solana replay" and two forecasting bounties
     (Financial: "Currency exchange, trading, and related services"; Gambling: "Sites or services providing tips,
     odds, handicapping, sports picks, forecasts"): hide them from ad traffic or clear them with X under item 1;
   - prints every bounty as a dollar amount: covered by item 1.
3. **The guarded-AI bounty on the board.** "Make a guarded AI reveal its secret" (URL slug `jailbreak-the-vault-01`)
   names the target model in its brief. It is one click from the landing page, and X's ToS §4 (iv) names
   "jailbreaking" and "prompt engineering or injection". These ads never mention, show or link it; keep it out of the
   ad-traffic view or include it in item 1's review, framed as make.fun frames it (published security findings). The
   agent guide `make.fun/skill.md` ("Earn USDC on Solana as an AI agent") belongs in the same review.
4. **Targeting.** 18+ only (make.fun's terms: "18 or older"; Prohibited Content for Minors). Exclude Canada unless X
   pre-authorizes it (Gambling, Canada: "Games are free to play but result in the payment of money or a prize of
   value." ... "Prior authorization from >x< is required."). Exclude "Belgium, Greece, Qatar, Russia, Singapore,
   Slovenia and Ukraine" (not available for crypto advertising) and any market item 1 does not clear. Target no
   sensitive category.
5. **Live-claim check, on upload day and through the flight** (Deceptive: "Promotion of offers or deals that are not
   available"). For this ad: At least one bounty is open on each day the ad runs.
6. **Promotions law.** Legal confirms skill contests with stablecoin awards comply in every targeted market (Guidelines

7. **Run as @makefunnyai ads, not creator posts.** A paid creator posting it needs the "Paid Partnership" disclosure,
