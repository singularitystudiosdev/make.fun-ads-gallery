## Preconditions before upload

These are advertiser-level conditions. No change to the creative can fix them, so they must be cleared before any
campaign goes live. Clauses were read live on 2026-11-08.

1. **Crypto payouts need X pre-authorization.** make.fun pays bounties in USDC on Solana (make.fun home, press kit,
   how-it-works). X's Financial Products and Services policy covers “cryptocurrency products and services”, and its
   Blockchain Games clause covers “games involving NFT or cryptocurrency tokens where such can be won or earned”:
   “Advertisers need to obtain a prior authorization from X by getting certified.” (US: “Blockchain games based on
   skill are permitted”.) X's policy does not say whether a free-to-enter, skill-graded coding competition that pays
   in a stablecoin counts as a blockchain game, so treat it as one. Apply at `https://ads.x.com/en/help/ads-pre-authorization`
   and get written clearance from the X account team before launch
   (`https://help.x.com/en/business-and-advertising/financial-services`).
2. **Landing page `https://make.fun/` must pass on its own.** The Quality policy says “The landing page must link to a
   working page and should be compliant with all ads policies” and “should not contain disruptive pop ups”. As read
   on 2026-11-08, the home page:
   - states every bounty as “$NNN reward, paid in USDC”. Item 1's authorization covers this.
   - lists “Beat our trading bots on a Solana replay”, which falls under Financial: “Currency exchange, trading”. It
     also lists two forecasting bounties (Roblox trends, Nasdaq moves), which fall under Gambling: “Sites or services
     providing tips, odds, handicapping, sports picks, forecasts”. The board's first carousel card is the trading
     bounty, with a Net PnL leaderboard in SOL. While ads run, take these off the home page, or get them approved by
     the X account team as part of item 1.
   - opens a first-visit “Hey, we're in beta!” sheet over the page. Turn it off for ad traffic, for example with a
     UTM flag.
3. **The featured bounty must be live on upload day** (Deceptive: “Promotion of offers or deals that are not
   available”). As read on 2026-11-08, “Make a guarded AI reveal its secret” closes 2026-11-10 01:00 UTC. “Write
   make.fun's tagline” and “Design make.fun's sticker” close 2026-11-11 01:00 UTC. If the bounty an ad names or shows
   has closed, re-skin the ad with a bounty that is live. It must also be non-financial and not a forecast.
4. **Targeting.** Set an 18+ age gate, because the ads promote crypto-paid competitions and minors must not be
   marketed gambling-like products (Prohibited Content for Minors). Exclude Canada unless X pre-authorizes it (Gambling,
   Canada: “Games are free to play but result in the payment of money or a prize of value ... Prior authorization from
   X is required”). Exclude the markets X lists as unavailable for crypto advertising (Belgium, Greece, Qatar,
   Russia, Singapore, Slovenia, Ukraine), and any market where item 1's certification is not granted.
5. **Promotions law.** Each bounty page carries its full rules, and “Entering is free: no purchase or payment is
   needed to enter or to win” (make.fun/how-it-works). Legal should confirm that skill contests comply in every
   targeted market (Guidelines for Promotions: “ensure that they comply with all applicable laws and regulations”).
6. **Run these as @makefunai ads, not creator posts.** If a creator or actor posts any of them organically for pay,
   the “Paid Partnership” disclosure and the FTC Endorsement Guides apply. In the EU, UK and Australia, crypto-related
   offers are not eligible for Paid Partnerships at all.
7. **Account eligibility and ads review.** The @makefunai ads account must pass X's account eligibility check and
   the X Ads review. Nothing here may be uploaded, posted or scheduled until items 1–4 are cleared.
