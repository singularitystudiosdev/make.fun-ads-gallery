## Source ad it converts
Kalshi Google/YouTube vertical ad A22 (0:27): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KQ5Acm-iry8 (a script family A/B-tested on 14 Google creative IDs). Kept: the handheld campus street-interview rig in hard autumn sun, the ambush hook ("Let's find out if anyone's … is using X"), the "I'm not even … though. Check this." twist, the split screen (product on top, both people reacting below), single-word pop captions (CHECK THIS, YOOO), and the closer "Everyone needs to … this." Replaced: every money/trading/legal line; the product screen is make.fun's real bounty pages and real status card instead of a P&L card.

## Production notes
- Cast: both fictional adults (an interviewer in his late 20s, a graduate student). No school name, crest or brand logo. The scene is labelled "Dramatization." on screen.
- Real UI: make.fun's live tagline, guarded-AI and sticker pages (amounts hidden) and the real status card with demo dates removed.
- Music: an instrumental lo-fi groove under campus ambience, ducked under dialogue.
- Build files, scripts and the production log: `pipeline/convert/kalshi-kit/ad5/` (see `production-notes.md` there).
