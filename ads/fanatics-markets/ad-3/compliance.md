## Preconditions before upload

Advertiser-level conditions the creative cannot settle. Do not upload until each is closed.

- **P1. Landing-page category ruling.** The Quality Policy says the landing page “should be compliant with all ads policies”. https://make.fun/ headlines “AI agents build software for cash rewards.”, lists every bounty with a dollar amount “paid in USDC”, and features a trading-bots bounty and two forecast bounties (“Predict tomorrow's top 10 trending Roblox games”, “Predict the next 5 trading days' moves of 9 Nasdaq stocks”). Under X's Financial Products and Services policy (“cryptocurrency products and services”; “Blockchain games ... where such can be won or earned”, which “need to obtain a prior authorization from X by getting certified”) and the Gambling policy (“forecasts”; “Games or apps played for money”), X may classify the destination as restricted. Before launch, get X's written ruling from Ads Support (@AdsSupport) or apply for pre-authorization (Financial Products & Services, and Gambling Content if X asks) at `https://ads.x.com/en/help/ads-pre-authorization`, giving https://make.fun/ as the URL. Pre-authorizations “must be approved before launching X Ads campaigns.”
- **P2. Landing-page pop-up.** On a first visit make.fun opens a “Hey, we're in beta!” dialog and a Discord sheet over the page (seen on the phone layout, 2026-11-08). The Quality Policy says “The landing page should not contain disruptive pop ups”. Suppress both for ad traffic (for example on a UTM-tagged visit) before launch.
- **P3. Targeting.** Adults only (18+, or the local age of majority where higher), because the destination runs paid contests and X bars marketing gambling to minors. Exclude markets where skill contests with prizes or crypto payouts are restricted, and the countries X lists as “not available for advertising cryptocurrency & DeFi products and services” (Belgium, Greece, Qatar, Russia, Singapore, Slovenia, Ukraine).
- **P4. Keep it current.** The MSA requires materials to “remain, current and accurate”. Run the ad only while make.fun has live bounties, and pull any bounty-specific version once the bounty it names has closed.
- **P5. Contest rules.** X's promotion guidelines ask for “a rule stating that anyone found to use multiple accounts to enter will be ineligible” and that contests “comply with all applicable laws and regulations”. Confirm make.fun's published rules carry the multiple-accounts rule and have been checked by counsel for each target market.
- **P6. Account eligibility and a fresh read.** The advertiser account (`@makefunai`) must pass X's ads approval. X's policies change, so the upload stage re-reads them on upload day.
