## Preconditions before upload (advertiser level; the creative cannot fix these)

Each is a blocker until it is done; none is a reason to soften the creative. Nothing has been uploaded, posted or scheduled.

1. **Crypto payouts: get X's Financial Products & Services pre-authorization first.** make.fun pays bounties in USDC on
   Solana (home page title "AI agent bounties paid in USDC on Solana"; How it works: "Top-ranked entrants claim USDC on
   Solana to their own wallet"). X's Financial policy covers "Blockchain games ... games involving NFT or cryptocurrency
   tokens where such can be won or earned", which are "only permitted once the advertiser meets country-specific
   licensing requirements", and "Advertisers need to obtain a prior authorization from X by getting certified."
   ([financial-services](https://help.x.com/en/business-and-advertising/financial-services),
   [pre-authorization](https://ads.x.com/en/help/ads-pre-authorization)). Whether a free-entry, skill-graded software
   bounty is a "game" is not defined on the page: treat it as covered and certify, or change the payout rail.
2. **Contest / prize review.** Gambling and Games lists "Games or apps played for money" and, for Canada, "Games are
   free to play but result in the payment of money or a prize of value ... Prior authorization from X is required"
   ([gambling-content](https://help.x.com/en/business-and-advertising/gambling-content)). Until X confirms the category:
   target the **United States only** (the Financial page's blockchain-games list, read in page order, places
   "Blockchain games based on skill are permitted" in the US slot; that mapping is inferred, not labelled), exclude
   Canada/Quebec, Brazil ("No gambling ads targeting Brazil are permitted"), India and Italy, and confirm the official
   rules meet US sweepstakes/contest law (X: "Compliance with such laws and regulations is your responsibility").
3. **Age.** Target 18+ only ("Prohibited Content for Minors": X prohibits marketing "Gambling products and services" to
   minors; a cash-paying contest may be read that way).
4. **Landing page must pass on its own** (Quality: "The landing page must link to a working page and should be
   compliant with all ads policies."; "The landing page should not contain disruptive pop ups"). `https://make.fun/` as
   read 2026-11-08 fails a strict read: its lead line is "AI agents build software for cash rewards.", its footer links
   "Make money with AI agents", it features "Beat our trading bots on a Solana replay" with a "$500" bounty and a
   leaderboard of SOL PnL and dollar rewards, and a first visit opens a "Hey, we're in beta!" dialog. Fix before upload:
   either land ad traffic on a make.fun page with no cash/"make money" lead, no trading or forecasting listings above
   the fold and no first-visit dialog, or hold the campaign until the Financial certification (1) covers this page.
5. **Build the website card in X Ads Manager** with this ad's own image and headline. Never post the bare URL: the
   site's own preview image (`/press/og-1200x630.png`) reads "Build it. Get paid.", which breaks the brief's
   no-money rule and X's "economic opportunities" line.
6. **Only run while the bounties shown are open** (Deceptive: "Promotion of offers or deals that are not available").
   Captured 2026-11-08: "Make a guarded AI reveal its secret" closes 2026-11-10 01:00 UTC; "Design make.fun's
   sticker" and "Write make.fun's tagline" close 2026-11-11 01:00 UTC. After that, re-capture the UI with
   `.tmp/pm-convert/tools/capture.mjs` against the then-live bounties (same redactions) and re-render, or pause the ad.
   A changed creative is a new ad and gets this review again.
7. **Post text never types the domain.** "make.fun" in post text auto-links as a URL, which Quality forbids
   ("Ads must not include hashtags or urls in the ad text"). Use "we", "our" or @makefunai.
8. **Advertiser account.** The @makefunai ads account must pass X's account eligibility and review (pages listed on the
   ads-policies index; not read in this pass), and the Master Services Agreement (https://ads.x.com/terms, not read
   in this pass) must be accepted by the account owner.
