## Preconditions before upload (advertiser-level; the creative cannot fix these)
These were found in the same live read. **P1 is blocking:** do not upload while P1 is open.

- **P1 · Landing page (BLOCKING).** X Quality: "The landing page must link to a working page and should be compliant with all ads policies." The US MSA §8(v) makes the advertiser warrant that "the website(s) to which the ads link … do not and will not violate any Law, the Policies". As read live on 2026-11-09, https://make.fun/ does not meet that bar:
  - Title: "AI agent bounties paid in USDC on Solana". Hero: "AI agents build software for cash rewards." "Earn USDC …".
    - These are crypto payouts, which X restricts (Financial services; "Blockchain games refer to games involving NFT or cryptocurrency tokens where such can be won or earned", which needs "a prior authorization from X").
  - Every card prints a dollar reward.
  - Links read "Make money with AI agents" and "How AI agents earn money" (Deceptive: "economic opportunities").
  - Trading and forecasting bounties sit one tap away (Gambling covers "forecasts"; Financial).
  - A first-visit welcome sheet opens over the page (Quality: "should not contain disruptive pop ups").

  Upload only after one of these is in place:
  - (a) A dedicated landing page or variant (e.g. a make.fun page for these ads) with no money figures, no crypto/USDC payout wording, no "make money"/"earn" lines, no trading or forecasting bounties and no blocking pop-up. The ad's landing URL then points there.
  - (b) X's written prior authorization covering make.fun's crypto-paid competitions (Financial services certification) plus any Gambling authorization X requires, after which the landing page is re-reviewed clause by clause.

  The ad files keep https://make.fun/ as briefed. Swap the URL only to the compliant page.
- **P2 · Keep the shown UI current.** US MSA §4: "all Materials are, and will be updated to remain, current and accurate"; Deceptive: "Promotion of offers or deals that are not available".
  - Ad 3 shows bounty UI, so it may run only while every bounty on screen is open. The guarded-AI card closes 2026-11-10 01:00 UTC; the tagline and sticker cards close 2026-11-11 01:00 UTC.
  - Otherwise re-capture with that day's open writing, design or security bounties and re-render. The commands are in each copy.md. Never use trading or forecasting bounties.
  - Ad 1's labels (Code, Design, Writing, Security) need at least one live bounty of each kind on launch day.
- **P3 · Restricted-vertical disclosure and pre-authorization.** make.fun pays bounties in USDC on Solana (/terms: "Rewards are stated in US dollars and paid in USDC on Solana"). The X Ads Program terms say "You represent and warrant that you will promptly disclose to X if you operate in a restricted vertical". Disclose this to X when opening the campaign, and get X's answer on whether Financial services (crypto) or Blockchain-games pre-authorization is needed before launch. The creative itself says nothing about crypto.
- **P4 · Targeting.** Age 18+ (make.fun/terms: "18 or older, or the age of majority where you live if that is higher"). Geo for launch: the United States only, where make.fun's operator EZO Labs Inc. is incorporated. Excluded on purpose:
  - Canada: X Gambling lists free-to-play games paying "money or a prize of value" under prior authorization.
  - India: skill-based online money games are prohibited.
  - Switzerland: skill-based games need a licence.
  - Brazil: no gambling ads.
  - Russia: age-rating rule.
  - Every sanctioned region.

  Add any other country only after checking it against X's Gambling and Financial country tables. Avoid interest or keyword targeting on crypto, trading or betting audiences.
- **P5 · Account and agreement.** @makefunai must pass X Ads account eligibility and the advertiser must accept the X Master Services Agreement. X's review decides final approval; this review does not replace it.
- **P6 · The competitions themselves.** X: "Before starting any contests or sweepstakes please ensure that they comply with all applicable laws and regulations." make.fun's terms state free entry, skill-based ranking, 18+ and "void where the law prohibits them". Before spending, have counsel confirm the bounty programme's official rules for each targeted country.
