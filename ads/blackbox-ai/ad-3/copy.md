## Production notes
- **Performer (fictional adult, about 30):** man with short tousled dark-brown hair and light stubble, plain unprinted rust-orange crew-neck tee, small unbranded black handheld mic, no glasses. Room lit warm amber with a soft magenta accent light, a shelf of plants and books behind (spines plain, nothing legible), slightly out of focus. No logos anywhere. Differs from the source creator on every axis (no glasses, no grey tee with print, no blue LED room, no monitor arm).
- **Still:** `nano-banana-pro-preview` (Google, via the media gateway), 9:16 2K, 2 candidates, #1 chosen (`ad3/performer_still.png`, the other kept as `still-2.png`). About $0.27.
- **Talking head:** `gemini-omni-1.1-flash` image_to_video, first frame = the still, 1080p, 6 s, speaking its own line: take A (0-5.17 s, hook) and take B (24.6-29.43 s, close), one take each, both accepted (no stutter, no baked-in text). About $0.92 each. Native 24 fps, converted to 30 fps by frame repeat.
- **Voice:** the Omni takes' own audio was converted with ElevenLabs speech-to-speech (`eleven_multilingual_sts_v2`, voice "Liam" `TX3LPaxmHKxFdv7VOQHJ`, background noise removed); the three screen-beat voiceover lines are ElevenLabs text-to-speech (`eleven_multilingual_v2`, same voice, speed 1.04, `tools/tts.py`), so one voice runs the whole film. Word timings from ElevenLabs Scribe (`scribe_v1`). Lip-sync lag measured by mouth-motion vs audio-envelope correlation (`scripts/lag.py`): the Omni video led its audio by 2 frames (A) and 3 frames (B), so the dialogue and its captions were advanced by 0.083 s and 0.125 s.
- **Screens (filmed-off-monitor look, built in post):** HTML scene rendered frame by frame in headless Chrome (`screens.html`): a monitor plane with perspective, a soft handheld sway, a slight blur, glare, vignette and grain, a warm room backdrop. Three real or original screens only; no UI was generated by a model.
  - Home box: fresh capture of https://make.fun/ on 2026-11-09 with headless Chrome (`cap_home.mjs`), dark theme; the beta sheet was dismissed with its own button and the neighbouring "Make money with AI agents" link hidden before the capture; the clip is the prompt box and its button only (so the line that names third-party tools and the USDC line above it are out of frame). The "Copied" state is the real button state after a real click.
  - Terminal: an original HTML terminal (JetBrains Mono), lines in the spirit of skill.md, no tool name, keypair, wallet, amount or bounty name. The sample commit id `4f9c2ab` is invented.
  - Status card: make.fun's real status-card markup from the frozen front end (`ui/status-card/`, lablab kit), dark theme, with the sentence under the title removed (it mentions "place") and the Challenge and Paid rows dropped, as the earlier kits did. The Checked state is the Submitted markup with its stage dots advanced.
  - Pointer: a small white mouse cursor drawn in the page (no hand). It glides to the real "Copy for your agent" button and clicks it, and the label switches to "Copied" on a single frame; there is no pointer on the status-card beat. Every scene change (camera -> box 5.17 s, box -> terminal 10.95 s, terminal -> status card 17.09 s, status card -> camera 24.6 s) is a hard cut, so no two screens ever overlap. A pointing-hand plate (one generated hand-on-green, `nano-banana-pro-preview`, about $0.14) was made and tried, then dropped because it read as a floating cutout; its cost stays in the estimate.
- **Caption timing check:** Scribe was run on the final encoded audio and every one of the 84 spoken words was compared with the caption word that reveals it: the largest difference is 0.02 s (no caption lags its word by more than 0.15 s).
- **Overlay:** title, Dramatization pill, captions and lockup tag are rendered by Chrome as transparent PNG frames (`overlay.html`) and composited by ffmpeg (`scripts/compose.sh`). Fonts: Inter (kit `InterVariable.woff2`), JetBrains Mono.
- **Music:** ElevenLabs Music (`music_v1`) instrumental plan, light upbeat acoustic bed at 112 bpm, mixed about 28 LU under the voice with side-chain ducking, 1.4 s fade at the end; two-pass loudnorm to -15 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP (`scripts/mix15.py`).
- **Takes and rejects:** one Omni take per beat (no re-rolls). Not used: fal.ai (every key returns 403 "Exhausted balance"); Grok i2v; Omni extend.
- **Review pass:** `gemini-4-argon` watched the finished film once (about $0.40); its verbatim transcript matched the script, with no baked-in text or logos. Its "audio ahead of the mouth by 1.5-2 s" remark was not reproduced by the signal measurement above and is put down to coarse timestamps.
- **Spend estimate:** about $2.9 media gateway (stills $0.27, hand $0.14, Omni 2 x $0.92 = $1.84, argon review $0.40, the earlier layout checks none) plus ElevenLabs credits (3 TTS lines of about 330 characters, 2 STS clips, 7 Scribe calls, one 34 s music track), well under $1. fal.ai: $0.
- **Assumptions:** the performer is a man (the source was too, but the look is new); the on-camera close runs 4.8 s and the opening 5.2 s because the lines need that long, which puts the title window at 4.45 s; "Scored" is shown as the real card's in-progress Scored state (no result, rank or place); the sandbox VO line says "runs every entry the same way, twice" as make.fun's how-it-works does; no bounty is named because all seven live bounties close by 2026-11-11 01:00 UTC.
- **Scratch:** `pipeline/convert/blackbox-ai-kit/ad3/` (stills, takes, audio, frames, screens.html, overlay.html, scripts, log.md).
