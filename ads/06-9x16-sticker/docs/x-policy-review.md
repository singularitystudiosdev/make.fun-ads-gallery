## What fixes each fail (priority order)

1. **Landing page: financial and blockchain-games rows (the blocker).** The ad promotes a crypto-paid contest product; get X financial certification for cryptocurrency products and services, or do not run it. A reduced campaign route (one coding bounty, no USDC/Solana/trading/earnings copy) clears the page content but not the category.
2. **Landing page: pop-up, relevance, forecasting/trading, staleness.** Serve a campaign route with no beta dialog, deep-linked to the "Close a month of books into a ledger that reconciles" bounty, with no trading or forecasting listings, re-captured on launch day.
3. **Film: staged win with no label.** Add a persistent "Demo. Results are staged." legend, move the win off frame 0, and soften "Climb to first." to "Aim for first."; put the scoring and "not every entry places" terms in the post copy.
4. **Film: leaderboard money residue.** Hide the "Reward" column header and cells and rename "Reward ranking" in the frozen DOM; sweep every frame for "Reward", "$", "USDC", "paid".
5. **Film: URLs lifted into stickers.** Keep `dev.make.fun/skill.md` and `https://github.com/you/month-close` only in the real UI fields; crop the URL out of the prompt sticker and type the repo value into the field instead of slapping a sticker.
6. **Film: clipped captions.** Hold every caption fully on screen before it leaves; no mid-word clipping at the frame edge. Re-check on the final render.
