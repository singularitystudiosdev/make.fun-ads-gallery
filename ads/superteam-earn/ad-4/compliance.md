## What was changed to pass
- Dropped Superteam's eligibility line ("No borders. No boundaries.") and the "High Paying Crypto Gig" headline.
- "Your next one might already be waiting" became "Its next build might already be waiting", so "next one" cannot be
  read as a promised win.
- No bounty is named or shown, so the ad cannot outlive a featured bounty.
- No URL or domain in the post text; the card is built by hand, never auto-unfurled.

## Preconditions before upload

Advertiser-level conditions no change to the creative can fix. Nothing may be uploaded, posted or scheduled until
items 1–4 are cleared.

1. **Crypto payouts need X pre-authorization.** make.fun pays bounties in USDC on Solana (its home title and
   how-it-works "Paid" stage). X's Financial Products and Services policy covers "cryptocurrency products and
   services", and its Blockchain Games clause covers "games involving NFT or cryptocurrency tokens where such can be
   won or earned": "Advertisers need to obtain a prior authorization from X by getting certified." Get that
   certification, or X's written word that none is needed, and target only the countries it covers
   (`https://help.x.com/en/business-and-advertising/financial-services`).
2. **The landing page https://make.fun/ must pass on its own.** As read on 2026-11-08 it shows a dollar amount on every
   bounty, "AI agents build software for cash rewards.", "paid in USDC on Solana" in its title, two trading bounties
   (Financial: "Currency exchange, trading"), two forecasting bounties (Gambling: "tips, odds, handicapping, sports
   picks, forecasts"), "Make money with AI agents" links, and a first-visit "Hey, we’re in beta!" sheet (Quality:
   "should not contain disruptive pop ups"). Fix these on the page for every visitor, X's reviewers included, or clear
   them with X under item 1. Never serve a cleaned page only to ad clicks (Deceptive, Circumventing Systems:
   "cloaking tactics … modifications to URL content following submission").
3. **Never let X auto-unfurl a make.fun link.** Every preview make.fun serves today carries "paid in USDC", "cash
   rewards" or an amount. Build the unit in X Ads as a Website Card with this ad's image (`creative.png` or
   `creative-1x1.png`), headline "Find your agent's next bounty" and button "Learn more"; keep URLs out of the post text.
4. **Targeting.** 18+ only. Exclude Canada unless X pre-authorizes it (Gambling, Canada: "Games are free to play but
   result in the payment of money or a prize of value … Prior authorization from X is required."). Exclude Belgium,
   Greece, Qatar, Russia, Singapore, Slovenia and Ukraine ("not available for advertising cryptocurrency & DeFi
   products and services"), India (Gambling, India: "online money games are prohibited … skill based" included) and
   every sanctioned country. Run only where item 1's certification applies.
5. **Claims stay true on air day.** "A board of open bounties" and "Free to enter" must be true each day the ad runs
   (MSA: Materials "will be updated to remain, current and accurate").
6. **Promotions law.** Legal confirms the skill bounties comply in every targeted market (Guidelines for Promotions:
   "ensure that they comply with all applicable laws and regulations").
7. **Run as a Promoted ad from make.fun's own account (@makefunnyai), never a creator post.** A paid creator post would
   need the Paid Partnership label and FTC disclosure, and crypto-related paid partnerships are not allowed in the EU,
   UK or Australia.
8. **Account eligibility and X's ad review.** The advertiser account must pass X's eligibility check and this ad X's
   review.
